Privacy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

En español, en corto: si creás una cuenta guardamos tu mail y tu avance en el curso, nada más. No vendemos ni compartimos tus datos con fines publicitarios. Lo que decís en los ejercicios de pronunciación lo procesa tu celular o tu navegador: no llega a nuestro servidor. Podés borrar tu cuenta cuando quieras desde Profile → Delete account.

Who is responsible

English on Board is made by Bianca Tortore, Berlin, Germany. Contact: biantortore@gmail.com.

What we keep, and why

  • Your account (only if you create one): your e-mail address, your name if you sign in with Google, and your progress in the course (lessons and sessions done, scores, tests, review list). We need this to give you the course and to keep your progress when you change phone.
  • Your certificate (only if you pass the final test and ask for it): the name you type, the date, your result and the certificate code. Anybody who has the code can check it at english.chartroomlab.com/verify, which shows that name, date and result.
  • Anonymous use of the app: a random number for this phone, the type of device, whether the app is installed, and which lessons and tests were done. It has no name or e-mail, and it helps us see which lessons work.
  • Feedback (only if you send it): what you write in the feedback form, and the name or contact you choose to add.

If you buy the full course we also keep the date of the purchase and its Paddle reference.

Your progress is also saved on your phone (browser storage), so the course works without signal at sea.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Your voice is not sent to us. The speaking exercises use the speech recognition of your phone or browser (made by Google or Apple), under their own privacy rules.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare: hosting of the app and storage of accounts and progress.
  • Resend: sends the e-mail with your sign-in code.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google", to confirm who you are. We receive your name and e-mail.
  • Paddle: only if you buy the full course. Paddle is our reseller and processes the payment; it receives your payment details and country, and tells us which account paid. We never see your card number.

Some of these companies may process data outside the European Union, under the protections the GDPR requires (such as the EU standard contractual clauses).

How long we keep it

Your account and progress are kept until you delete your account. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. Certificates stay valid so that they can be checked, unless you ask us to remove yours.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its use. You can delete your account yourself in the app (Profile → Delete account), or write to us. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in Berlin this is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.
