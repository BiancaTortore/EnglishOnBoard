ChartroomLab · English on Board

Maritime English for Spanish-speaking deck officers

A self-paced course in a phone app. Made by Bianca Tortore, deck officer.

En español: un curso de inglés marítimo para oficiales y cadetes de puente hispanohablantes. Seguís a Diego, un cadete argentino, en su primer viaje en un buque de LNG, de Houston a Marsella. Escuchás, hablás y practicás con situaciones reales de a bordo. Funciona en el celular, también sin señal.

What you get

27 lessons in 7 modules
Signing on, safety, the bridge watch, cargo, port State control, emergencies, radio, pilot and helm orders.
Real voices
Every dialogue recorded with a cast of characters: captain, officers, bosun, pilot, VTS, port inspector.
Speaking practice
Say it out loud and the app checks your pronunciation. Radio messages in standard maritime phrases.
Works at sea
Download the course once and use it without signal on board.
Tests and certificate
A test after each module, a final test and a certificate of completion with a code anybody can check.
Your account
Your progress is saved in your account and comes with you to a new phone.

What it is, and what it isn't

  • It is a digital, self-paced course: there are no live classes and no teacher assigned to you.
  • It is a language course. It is not approved by the IMO and it is not an STCW certificate.
  • Module 1 is free so you can try it first. Purchases are not refundable once the access starts. See Refunds.
