## `hero.jpg` · portada de la lección
- **Formato:** 4:5 vertical, mínimo 1080 × 1350.

> Photograph from a low angle on the deck of a ship: in the foreground, slightly out of focus, the shoulder and white safety helmet of a young man seen from behind [reference: diego]; he looks up at the enormous curved white wall of a spherical tank dome that fills most of the frame and rises against a clear morning sky. Only the smooth white curve of the dome and the sky are visible, no pipes, no equipment, no railings in focus. Soft golden morning light. Documentary photograph, 28mm lens, realistic, no visible text, no logos, no watermark.

## `scene.jpg` · el diálogo (sesión Listen)
- **Formato:** 4:3 horizontal, mínimo 1440 × 1080.

> Medium shot at eye level on the deck of a ship. A 46-year-old Filipino bosun [reference: rolando] in a worn orange rating's boiler suit and white helmet points up and forward, out of the frame, explaining with a smile. Next to him a 22-year-old Argentine young man [reference: diego], still in jeans and a dark jacket but now wearing a white visitor's safety helmet, looks up where the bosun points, curious. Behind them, completely out of focus, the white curve of a huge spherical tank dome and a white superstructure. No ropes, no equipment, no railings in focus. Morning light. Documentary photograph, 50mm lens, natural expressions, realistic, no visible text, no logos, no watermark.
