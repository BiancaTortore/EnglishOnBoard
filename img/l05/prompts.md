## `scene.jpg` · el diálogo (sesión Listen)

> Medium shot on a ship's bridge in port, daytime. The large bridge windows behind show a gas terminal with cranes and pipework, slightly out of focus. A 33-year-old Irish man with red hair and a short red beard [reference: james], in a navy fleece, stands next to a 22-year-old Argentine young man [reference: diego] in a plain navy-blue boiler suit. James hands him a black radio handset with a coiled cord and smiles, about to leave; Diego takes it, a little nervous, a notebook and pen in his other hand. The radio and console are out of focus at the edge of the frame; no screens or readable text. Documentary photograph, 35mm lens, natural expressions, realistic, no logos, no watermark.
